FAQ

Questions before we go live?

Do we need a studio or special equipment?

No. Many webinars can be produced with remote presenters using a good webcam, microphone and stable internet connection. We can also support studio-based productions.

Do you produce webinars through Zoom or Teams?

Yes. The production can be built around Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or another delivery platform depending on your event.

Can you use our existing brand assets?

Yes. Logos, presentation decks, videos and brand guidelines can be integrated into the production. We can also create additional graphics when needed.

Can you support speakers in different locations?

Yes. Remote presenters can be incorporated into the production workflow with pre-event technical checks.

What determines the final price?

The final quote depends on duration, number of presenters, number of sources/cameras, graphic complexity, moderation requirements, delivery platform and post-production needs.
